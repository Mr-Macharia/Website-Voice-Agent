# About Gichogu Macharia

## Short version

Gichogu Macharia is an AI Engineer and AI Strategist based in Nairobi, Kenya.
He builds AI-enabled systems, backend services, automation workflows and
data-driven tools — combining hands-on engineering with strategy and product
thinking. He is currently an AI Engineer at Visiondrill, where he was promoted
to Lead Developer, and runs his own consulting practice, Gich AI Nexus.

## Longer version

Gichogu works at the intersection of AI, strategy and development. His own
framing is that AI is a tool and strategy is the blueprint, and that the two
together are what separate ideas that survive from ideas that succeed.

His background is deliberately cross-functional. He holds a BSc in Strategic
Management from Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology (JKUAT),
alongside training in Data Science at Moringa School and Data and AI at Cyber
Shujaa. That blend of business grounding and technical depth means he
approaches AI as a strategist as well as an engineer.

Day to day his work spans applied AI engineering, retrieval-augmented
generation, backend API development, reporting and dashboard support, process
automation and client solutions. He has worked in both structured
organisational roles and independent consulting, which has given him a
practical understanding of delivery, experimentation, documentation and
cross-functional collaboration.

Beyond engineering, he consults on AI adoption, business optimisation and AI
training. His focus there is on helping businesses understand not just what AI
can do today, but what it will demand of them over the next five years.

He is strongest where technical build work, product usefulness and business
context need to come together — an applied builder rather than a narrowly
siloed specialist.

## What he cares about

Building AI that is impactful, ethical, and aligned with real-world goals,
particularly in the African context. He writes and speaks about Kenya's
national AI strategy, data sovereignty, and making sure AI adoption is
inclusive rather than widening existing gaps.

## Role positioning

Depending on the context, Gichogu can credibly be described as an AI Engineer,
Applied AI Engineer, AI Strategist, AI Platform Engineer, AI Automation
Specialist, Backend and AI Systems Developer, Full-Stack AI Systems Builder, or
Retrieval-Augmented Generation Engineer.

## Working style

Practical, adaptable, collaborative and delivery-oriented. He is strongest in
roles where technical work has to be translated into something usable,
explainable and valuable to a business, team, learner group or end user, and in
environments that value initiative, experimentation, documentation, mentoring
and cross-functional contribution.
